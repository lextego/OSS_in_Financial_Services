# Open Source Financial Services Capability Catalogue

Welcome to the **Open Source Financial Services Capability Catalogue** repository! This document provides a curated, consolidated view of open-source solutions aligned with key financial service capabilities, drawing from established industry taxonomies such as BIAN, ISO 20022, ACORD, and FINOS.

The list has been curated for [OpenUK](https://openuk.uk/), by LexTego and other contributors, to support and advance the adoption of open-source software within the UK's financial services industry. OpenUK promotes the growth and sustainability of open-source technologies by providing resources, guidance, and advocacy to institutions and individuals committed to open principles. This catalogue aligns with OpenUK's mission by offering a practical and accessible resource for organizations looking to integrate robust, secure, and innovative open-source solutions into their operational and technological frameworks.

*We have used ChatGPT to accelerate the creation and structure of the list, but validate any details to ensure that real products are referenced*

## Purpose

This catalogue serves as a foundational reference for financial institutions, developers, architects, and industry specialists to quickly identify robust open-source projects aligned with essential business capabilities, streamlining the integration of these solutions into their technology ecosystems.

## Structure

The repository is organized into capability groups derived from trusted financial services taxonomies, categorized into these core functional areas:

* **[Customer & Channels](01_customer_and_channels.md)**
* **[Products & Accounts](02_products_and_accounts.md)**
* **[Origination & Servicing](03_origination_and_servicing.md)**
* **[Payments & Transfers](04_payments_and_transfers.md)**
* **[Trading & Investment](05_trading_and_investment.md)**
* **[Treasury & Liquidity](06_treasury_and_liquidity.md)**
* **[Risk, Finance & Compliance](07_risk_finance_and_compliance.md)**
* **[Data & Analytics](08_data_and_analytics.md)**
* **[Enterprise Services](09_enterprise_services.md)**
* **[Reference Data Appendix](10_reference_data.md)**

Each area includes examples of mature, community-supported open-source solutions for practical evaluation and adoption.

## How to Contribute

We warmly invite the community to enhance this catalogue. You can contribute by:

* Adding new open-source projects that fit into the capability categories.
* Correcting inaccuracies or outdated information.
* Suggesting improvements to the categorization or structure.

### Submitting a Change Request

To suggest additions or corrections:

1. Fork this repository.
2. Create a new branch (`git checkout -b your-feature-branch`).
3. Make your changes and commit them (`git commit -am 'Added project XYZ to Payments & Transfers'`).
4. Push your changes (`git push origin your-feature-branch`).
5. Create a Pull Request to the main repository.

## Evaluation Criteria for Inclusion

Projects listed in this catalogue typically meet the following criteria:

* Openly licensed (e.g., Apache 2.0, MIT, GPL).
* Actively maintained with recent commits and community support.
* Demonstrable use cases and production references within financial services.
