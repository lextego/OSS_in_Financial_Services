
Here’s a quick-start catalogue of openly-licensed projects you can map to each Level-1 capability.  I’ve picked 2-3 mature, community-supported options per row so you can mix-and-match as your architecture evolves.

| Level-1 Area                   | Open-source products you can evaluate                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Customer & Channels**        | • **SuiteCRM** – SugarCRM fork that gives you full-stack CRM with REST/GraphQL APIs for call-centre, branch or adviser tooling. ([SuiteCRM][1])  <br>• **Odoo (Community Edition)** – modular ERP/CRM with web & mobile front-ends; many FSI’s use it for digital onboarding flows. ([Odoo][2])                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Products & Accounts**        | • **Apache Fineract** – cloud-ready core-banking & ledger engine with full accounting and product-factory support. ([fineract.apache.org][3]) <br>• **Stellar Core** – high-throughput distributed ledger for deposit, savings and wallet balances (ISO 20022 bridges available). ([developers.stellar.org][4]) <br>• **Moov Accounts** – Go-based general-ledger micro-service that plugs into ACH/Card rails or your own RTP switch. ([GitHub][5]) |
| **Origination & Servicing**    | • **OpenCBS Loan Origination** – workflow-driven LOS + underwriting that integrates with any CBS. ([opencbs.com][6]) <br>• **Drools** – battle-tested rules/DMN engine used for real-time credit decisioning and claims triage. ([GitHub][7]) <br>• **OpenL Tablets** – Excel-style business-rules platform (GPL) often embedded for pricing and policy servicing. ([GitHub][7])                                                                     |
| **Payments & Transfers**       | • **Mojaloop** – instant-payment hub/RTGS switch with ISO 20022 FSPIOP APIs; live in several national schemes. ([misp-project.org][8]) <br>• **Interledger Protocol (ILP)** – open routing protocol for cross-ledger/cross-border value packets. ([Kill Bill][9]) <br>• **Kill Bill** – extensible billing & payment orchestration platform with plugins for major gateways, cards and ACH. ([Kill Bill][9])                                         |
| **Trading & Investment**       | • **QuickFIX / QuickFIX-J / Go** – de-facto OSS FIX engine family for OMS/EMS connectivity.  <br>• **QuantLib** – quantitative library covering pricing, curve-building and risk analytics.  <br>• **OpenGamma Strata** – Java toolkit for market data, margin, collateral & trade models.                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Treasury & Liquidity**       | • **Open Source Risk Engine (ORE)** – ALM, XVA and funding-cost analytics built on QuantLib.  <br>• **QuantLib** – cash-flow forecasting, curve construction and FX/MM pricing primitives.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Risk, Finance & Compliance** | • **OpenGamma Strata** – VaR, SIMM and scenario analytics for market & counterparty risk.  <br>• **MISP Threat Sharing** – platform for sharing fraud/AML indicators and cyber IOCs across institutions. ([Wikipedia][10]) <br>• **Drools + DMN** – also widely used for AML rule-sets, regulatory reporting validations and IFRS-9 triggers. ([GitHub][7])                                                                                          |
| **Data & Analytics**           | • **Apache Kafka** – distributed event-stream backbone for reference-data hubs and lineage capture.  <br>• **Apache Superset** – self-service BI dashboards on top of any SQL lake/warehouse.  <br>• **Kubeflow** – Kubernetes-native ML workbench (feature-store, pipelines, model-serving).                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Enterprise Services**        | • **Keycloak** – SSO, OAuth 2/OIDC & IAM with LDAP/AD federation.  <br>• **Camunda Platform / Zeebe** – BPMN/DMMN workflow engine for end-to-end process orchestration.  <br>• **Prometheus** – metrics/alerting stack that underpins cloud observability for micro-services.                                                                                                                                                                        |

**How to use this list**

* Pick an area, identify the functional gaps in your current stack, then prototype with one of the libraries or full-stack platforms shown.
* Most communities provide Docker images, Helm charts or Terraform modules for easy evaluation.
* Ensure licence compatibility (Apache 2.0, AGPL, GPL, etc.) with your deployment model before moving to production.

Happy building!
